Brainard Philemon Jagati

I am a final-year Computer Science undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Nagpur, advised by Prof. Jitendra Tembhurne. I am currently a visiting student researcher at the Singapore University of Technology and Design under Prof. Ezekiel Soremekun. At Nanyang Technological University, I work on hallucination mitigation strategies in medical LLMs under Prof. Erik Cambria.

Interests: AI Safety · AI Alignment · Mechanistic Interpretability · Red Teaming · Representation Engineering

I started out in representation learning, drawn to the latent spaces that neural networks learn. My internships with Prof. Soremekun and Prof. Cambria then gave me the chance to explore AI safety and red-teaming techniques. Since the recent OpenAI–Hugging Face incident, I have become especially concerned with how models behave when they are misaligned. I want to study this behavior through mechanistic interpretability, to better understand why models act the way they do.
